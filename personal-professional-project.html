Personal Professional Project

Career Vision · Networking · Industry Connections

Connecting with professionals is one of the best ways to prepare effectively for entering the job market after my studies. I use platforms such as LinkedIn and MyJobGlasses to reach out to engineers and managers working in the transportation and aeronautics sector.

Thanks to MyJobGlasses, I had the opportunity to exchange with professionals from major companies: my internship at TE Connectivity and inspiring discussions with two professionals from Thales in the aeronautics and aerospace domain. These conversations helped me better understand the daily reality of engineers and the expectations of large industrial groups.

Career Interest

✈️

Transportation & Aeronautics

Aviation Data ATM / ATC Big Data Data Science Cloud Engineering

Aeronautics and aerospace fascinate me because of their extraordinary technological challenges and their central contribution to global mobility. The transportation sector has a strong societal impact, combining innovation, safety, and high performance — a perfect match for my passion for applied mathematics, data engineering and machine learning. My goal is to contribute to the digital transformation of air traffic management and aeronautical systems, as illustrated by my data science internship at DGAC/DSNA.
