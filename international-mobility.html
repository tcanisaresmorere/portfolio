▸ About this Exchange

As part of my Semester 9 at ENSEEIHT, I completed an Erasmus+ exchange semester at the University of Stavanger (UiS), Norway. This experience was a natural extension of my specialization in HPC & Big Data, deepening my expertise in Cloud Computing and Data Engineering in an international academic environment.

Living in Norway allowed me to fully immerse myself in an English-speaking professional context, engage with international students and faculty, and experience Scandinavian innovation culture — closely aligned with my career ambitions in aeronautical data systems and advanced analytics.

🎯 Academic & Professional Goals

The University of Stavanger offered a strong program in data-oriented engineering with courses directly complementary to my specialization at ENSEEIHT. Cloud Computing and Data Engineering skills acquired during this semester are highly valuable for my target career in aeronautical data systems (ATM data lakes, ADS-B processing pipelines, cloud-native analytics). The immersive English environment also significantly strengthened my ability to work in international, multicultural teams — a key asset for a career at companies such as Airbus.
