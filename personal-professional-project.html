Personal Professional Project

Aeronautics Vocation · DSNA Experience · Career Vision

My Personal Professional Project is entirely oriented towards one goal: building a career in the aeronautics sector. This ambition was not built overnight — it has been shaped by a hands-on internship, active research into the industry, and enriching exchanges with professionals who work in it every day.

From my immersion in air traffic data at the DGAC/DSNA to my ongoing research into flight efficiency and decarbonation, every step of this project has confirmed the same conclusion: aeronautics is where I want to build my engineering career.

Aeronautics & Air Traffic Management

Aviation Data ATM / ATC Big Data Data Science Cloud Engineering

Aeronautics fascinates me because of its extraordinary technological challenges and its central role in global mobility. It is a sector where innovation, safety, and high performance meet — a perfect match for my passion for applied mathematics, data engineering and machine learning. My goal is to contribute to the digital transformation of air traffic management and aeronautical systems, building on the direction confirmed by my data science internship at DGAC/DSNA.

My DSNA Internship — A Vocation Confirmed

Data Office · DGAC/DSNA, Toulouse

My internship at the DSNA Data Office was the turning point of my Personal Professional Project. Being immersed on a daily basis in real ADS-B trajectory data, working alongside air traffic management (ATM) experts, and tackling concrete operational and environmental problems gave me a first-hand understanding of what a career in aeronautical data science actually looks like — and it convinced me this is exactly the environment I want to work in.

Beyond the technical work, this experience showed me how data science can directly support safer, more efficient and more sustainable air traffic — confirming that I want to keep building my career at the intersection of Big Data and aeronautics.
